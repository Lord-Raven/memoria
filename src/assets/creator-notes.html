Stage Details

This bot is little more than a container for a stage which replaces Chub's typical chat interface with a VN framework set in the world of Ardeia.

The story unfolds through a series of skits that are shaped by past summaries, dynamic lorebook entries, and the user's choices. Messages can be edited or re-rolled like a chat, but once a skit is ended, its events are locked in. The stage then generates a summary and makes updates to associated lorebook entries to inform future skits.

This stage makes many additional behind-the-scenes LLM calls, which may utilize additional tokens or credits—please bear that in mind.
